Room Read
dance caller for Android

Which dance is this?

Room Read listens to the music in the room and says the dance in your earpiece: waltz, cha cha, salsa, Lindy hop and fifteen more, ballroom, Latin and social.

It works entirely on your phone. It has no account, no adverts and no internet access, and it never records what it hears.
