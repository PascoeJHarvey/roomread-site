Room Read
dance caller for Android

Test Room Read

Room Read hears the music in the room and says the dance in your ear. Joining the test takes about 5 minutes.

Video: A short video of these steps is coming soon.

You need

  • An Android phone with Android 8.0 or newer, 64-bit. Most phones from the last few years are fine.
  • A Google account: the one your phone's Play Store uses.
  • About 2 GB of free space. The download is about 520 MB, because the library of dance recordings comes with the app. After that it works offline.

Sign in with your Play Store account. Google's pages below may show Sign in at the top right, or say you don't have permission. That just means you're not signed in yet: sign in with the Google account your phone's Play Store uses (in Play Store, tap your profile picture to see which). Easiest: open this page on your phone, in Chrome signed into that account.

1Join the testers' group

Open the testers' group

https://groups.google.com/g/roomread-testers/about

If the page shows Sign in at the top right, sign in first, with the same Google account your phone's Play Store uses. Then tap Join group.

The group has no messages, so a page saying you don't have permission to see conversations is normal: use the About page and its Join group button.

Members can't see each other, and the group sends no emails.

2Accept the test invitation

Open the test invitation

https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.pascoeharvey.roomread

Tap Become a tester, signed in with that same account. The page then shows a link to Room Read on Google Play, or use the button in step 3.

3Install Room Read

Open Room Read on Google Play

https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pascoeharvey.roomread

Install it from Google Play on your phone. It can take a few minutes after joining before Play offers it.

While it's in testing, Play may call it com.pascoeharvey.roomread (unreviewed) or Room Read. Both are this app.

4Open it and start listening

  1. Open Room Read. It explains why it needs the microphone. Allow the microphone.
  2. Allow notifications, so you can see it and stop it from the notification.
  3. Put your earpiece in, tap Start listening and put the phone in your pocket.

It announces the dance of each new song.

To stop, tap Stop in the app or in the notification. Swiping the app away from recent apps also stops it.

In the settings you can choose how sure the dance model has to be, or switch the model off.

Please stay in the test for at least 14 days. Google needs at least 12 testers for 14 days before the app can be released publicly, so leaving early resets the count.

If something goes wrong

  • "Item not found" or "App not available". Check, in order: (1) you tapped Become a tester on the test invitation page (step 2): joining the group alone isn't enough; (2) your phone's Play Store is signed into the same Google account (in Play Store, tap your profile picture; switch account there if needed); (3) you opened Room Read with the button in step 3 or the link on the invitation page: searching the Play Store won't find a test app; (4) it has been a few minutes: after joining, Google can take up to an hour to let you in; (5) your phone has Android 8.0 or newer and is 64-bit: on older phones Play won't offer it. Still stuck? Email roomreadthecaller@gmail.com with your phone's make and model.
  • A link opens in another browser or account. Copy the link and paste it into Chrome, signed into the right account.
  • Not enough space. Free up about 2 GB.

Send feedback

Email feedback

roomreadthecaller@gmail.com

Songs it called wrong or missed help most. Say the song, the artist, what it said and roughly when.

You can also leave private feedback on the app's Play page. Only the developer sees it during testing.

Privacy

Room Read has no account, no adverts, no analytics and no internet permission. Nothing it hears is recorded or sent anywhere.
